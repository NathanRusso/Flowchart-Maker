How It Works

  • Select a GCCIS degree template to load a prebuilt flowchart.
  • Drag and drop courses between semesters to match your academic plan.
  • Fill elective and course option slots using the provided text boxes and dropdown menus.
  • Add or remove years and manage transfer courses as needed.
  • Save your flowchart locally and reload it later to continue editing.
  • Click on a course to view more information, including its full name, number of credits, and description.
  • For a more in-depth explanation, visit the About page.
